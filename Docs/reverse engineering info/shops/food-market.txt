mm.exe file:
food market algorithm
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
food market algorithm is a part of mm.exe.
Offset of code is 0x1dfc.
There are 5 food markets (one per town)

Food cost (sorpigal, portsmit, algary, dusk, erliquin):
0x5, 0xA, 0x14, 0xC8, 0x32
 
Every character buys food upto 40 food if he/she has enough gold.

interesting findings:
**********************************
a character always pays full price. Does not matter how many food he has. Even if he has food>=40  he pays full price for nothing.
